# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cosmic Beatmaker primarily serves curious non-musicians who enjoy creative apps, music visualizers, games, and sandbox tools but find traditional music software intimidating. They use it on phones and desktops to make a satisfying loop quickly through direct manipulation, without first learning theory or a DAW workflow. Musicians and game creators are a secondary audience who need predictable rules, seedable variation, deeper pattern and harmony controls, and useful exports.

## Product Purpose

Cosmic Beatmaker turns a miniature solar system into a four-bar groovebox: the star establishes mood and harmony, planets play musical roles, moons embellish them, rings create regular rhythmic textures, and asteroids add controlled irregularity. Success means a first-time user hears a coherent groove within 30 seconds, makes a meaningful edit within one minute, understands which object caused each audible event, and can save, share, and export the result.

## Brand Personality

Playful, legible, empowering. The experience should feel like discovering a responsive musical toy with the trustworthiness of a focused creative instrument: imaginative without becoming cryptic, energetic without becoming frantic, and sophisticated without exposing complexity too early.

## Anti-references

- A traditional DAW reskinned with stars, dense timelines, mixer channels, and unexplained theory terminology.
- An empty, silent sandbox that asks beginners to construct the whole system before hearing anything.
- A neon-purple sci-fi control panel whose decorative effects obscure musical state or reduce mobile legibility.
- A game HUD with tiny targets, gesture-only commands, or constant animated spectacle.
- A fully automatic generator that hides causality and gives the user no ownership of the result.

## Design Principles

- Begin with authored sound: every new user starts from a coherent, audible system rather than a blank canvas.
- Make sound visible: every note, hit, pulse, and fill has an identifiable celestial cause.
- Reveal depth on demand: plain-language macros and role choices lead; detailed patterns and theory follow.
- Treat touch and accessibility as primary input models: every essential canvas action has a semantic DOM alternative.
- Protect experimentation: safe harmony, bounded generation, undo, stable IDs, and versioned saves make exploration low-risk.
- Preserve the clock: rendering quality can adapt or fail without affecting musical timing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the HTML interface. Essential actions must be keyboard reachable, visibly focused, and exposed through semantic controls with targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels. State cannot rely on color alone. The app must respect browser zoom and reduced-motion preferences and provide explicit reduced-particle, reduced-flash, master-stop, and master-volume controls. Canvas manipulation must be mirrored by an accessible object list and tap/button alternatives, and screen-reader announcements should describe major edits without announcing every musical event.
