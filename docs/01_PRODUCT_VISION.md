# Product Vision

## Product definition

Cosmic Beatmaker is a **mobile-first 3D groovebox** where users create a looping piece of music by building a miniature solar system.

The star establishes the system's musical mood. Planets become major musical parts. Moons add related accents and harmonies. Rings create stable rhythmic textures. Asteroid belts add loose, probabilistic percussion. Orbital movement visualizes the timing of every event.

The app is not a traditional DAW presented in space. It is a new visual instrument with a deliberately limited, coherent musical language.

## One-line pitch

> Build a solar system. Make a beat. No music theory required.

## Expanded pitch

Cosmic Beatmaker turns music sequencing into a playful spatial experience. Add a beat planet, orbit a bass world around a star, attach moons for accents, and break pieces out of a planetary ring to shape a hi-hat rhythm. Beginner-safe scales, progressions, rhythm templates, and automatic mixing make the first result satisfying, while deeper pattern, harmony, sound, and export controls give musicians useful creative material.

## Target audience

### Primary: curious non-musicians

People who enjoy creative apps, music visualizers, games, or sandbox tools but find conventional music software intimidating.

They need:

- Immediate sound
- Plain-language choices
- Strong musical guardrails
- Obvious visual feedback
- Easy undo
- Low fear of making mistakes

### Secondary: musicians and game creators

People who want an unusual sketching instrument for grooves, motifs, soundtrack ideas, or generative inspiration.

They need:

- Predictable musical rules
- Detailed pattern editing
- Harmony controls
- Seeded mutation
- MIDI and audio export
- Enough control to preserve useful ideas

### Tertiary: casual spectators and sharers

People who may mostly explore generated systems, use performance mode, and share seeds or recordings.

## Problem

Traditional music tools usually begin with timelines, grids, terminology, and routing. Many people are interested in making music but do not know where to start or fear that every action will sound wrong.

At the other extreme, fully automatic generators can produce polished results while giving the user little ownership over the musical construction.

Cosmic Beatmaker provides a third path:

- Direct manipulation instead of a blank piano roll
- Guardrails instead of unrestricted randomness
- Visible musical causality instead of opaque generation
- Exportable structure instead of disposable output

## Core promise

A new user should be able to create a coherent, personal loop around a four-bar harmony phrase within sixty seconds. Individual planets may repeat at shorter, longer, or polymetric rates while the interface derives their complete synchronization boundary.

A returning user should continue discovering deeper relationships between rhythm, harmony, orbits, satellites, and sound.

## Product pillars

### 1. Instant musical success

The default system uses consonant harmony, curated rhythm grammar, register separation, automatic balance, and safe mutation.

### 2. Visible causality

Every note, hit, accent, filter pulse, or fill has a visible object and event associated with it.

### 3. Progressive depth

The initial surface uses roles, moods, and macro controls. Music theory, detailed patterns, probabilities, voicings, and effects are revealed only when requested.

### 4. Touch-first creation

The product must be comfortable on a phone. Canvas gestures are simple, controls are large, and all essential functions remain available through ordinary accessible HTML UI.

### 5. Useful ownership

Users can save, duplicate, seed, share, export audio, and export structured MIDI. The result belongs to their creative workflow.

### 6. Delightful performance

The solar system should be enjoyable to watch. Motion, pulses, light, particles, and camera behavior reinforce the music without obscuring editing.

## Focus statement

Cosmic Beatmaker is specifically:

> A four-bar-phrase cosmic groovebox with visible polymetric orbits, optimized for fast, safe, tactile loop creation.

It is not trying to model full songs during the Build Week version.

## Success measures

### User experience

- A first-time user hears a complete groove within 30 seconds.
- A first-time user makes a meaningful edit within 60 seconds.
- A user can identify which object is producing a sound.
- The primary flow is usable with touch only.
- Undo is always available after creative mutations.

### Creative value

- Generated systems sound intentional rather than randomly populated.
- Different star and mood presets produce clearly different identities.
- Users can preserve one idea while regenerating another.
- MIDI export contains distinct, sensibly named tracks.

### Technical quality

- Audio remains stable during frame-rate drops.
- Mid-tier mobile devices remain responsive.
- Shared systems reproduce deterministically.
- The app can be deployed as a static web application.

## Non-goals

The Build Week version will not be:

- A complete DAW
- A realistic orbital simulation
- A song-length linear arranger
- A multiplayer jam room
- An account-based social platform
- A hardware MIDI workstation
- A procedural planet terrain simulator
- A WebGPU showcase
- A product with live generative AI
- A massive sample marketplace
